Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy completely. Use this page to request the deletion of any personal data associated with your account or purchase.

What We Collect

Your Data at Revitalize and Thrive Now

When you purchase a product from our Gumroad store, Gumroad collects your name, email address, and payment information to process your order. We may receive your name and email address to fulfill your order and send you your digital product.

We do not store credit card numbers, sell your personal information, or share your data with third parties beyond what is required to process your purchase and deliver your product.

How to Request Deletion

Your Deletion Options

  • 1
    Submit the form below — Fill in your name and the email address used at purchase. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm via email.
  • 2
    Email us directly — Send a deletion request to hello@alexanderwellnessreimagined.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and your purchase email address.
  • 3
    Request via Gumroad — Since purchases are processed through Gumroad, you may also submit a data deletion request directly at gumroad.com/privacy.
