# Reclaim & Rise Now — Multi-Platform Video Strategy

## Platform Requirements & Specifications

### 1. INSTAGRAM REELS
- **Duration:** 15-90 seconds (optimal: 21-34 seconds)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920px minimum
- **Format:** MP4 or MOV
- **Max File Size:** 100MB
- **Codec:** H.264 video, AAC audio
- **Best Practice:** Hook in first 3 seconds, CTA in last 5 seconds

### 2. TIKTOK
- **Duration:** 15 seconds - 10 minutes (optimal: 21-34 seconds)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920px minimum
- **Format:** MP4 or MOV
- **Best Practice:** Trend-aligned, fast-paced cuts, on-screen text, sound critical

### 3. YOUTUBE SHORTS
- **Duration:** 15-60 seconds
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920px minimum
- **Format:** MP4 or MOV
- **Best Practice:** Self-contained story, strong hook, CTAs to full videos

### 4. YOUTUBE FULL-LENGTH
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes (optimal for conversion content)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (horizontal)
- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) minimum, 4K preferred
- **Format:** MP4
- **Best Practice:** Multi-scene, B-roll, testimonials, detailed product walkthrough

## Video Production Strategy

### Option 1: Higgsfield Video Generation (Current Approach)
**Pros:**
- AI-generated professional actors
- Consistent branding
- No production crew needed
- Scalable

**Cons:**
- Credit-based (currently exhausted)
- Limited customization
- May need multiple takes for best quality

**Best For:** Quick turnaround, high-volume production

### Option 2: Stock Video + B-Roll + Text Overlays
**Approach:**
- Use Pexels/Unsplash for base footage (free)
- Add motion graphics, text overlays, captions
- Include voiceover or licensed music
- Build in CapCut or similar

**Pros:**
- No credits needed
- Infinite customization
- Professional final product

**Cons:**
- Manual production time
- Requires editing skills/tool

**Best For:** Custom messaging, detailed product education

### Option 3: Hybrid Approach (RECOMMENDED)
1. **Use Higgsfield** for hero character videos (when credits available)
2. **Use stock footage** for supporting B-roll
3. **Add text overlays, graphics, captions** for messaging
4. **Include voiceover** from tools like ElevenLabs
5. **Composite in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve**

**Result:** Professional multi-scene videos without full production crew

## Platform-Specific Optimization

### INSTAGRAM REELS Strategy
- **Hook:** First 0.5 seconds = viewer decision
- **Text Overlay:** Problem statement
- **Pacing:** Fast cuts every 1-2 seconds
- **CTA:** "Tap link in bio" + URL in captions
- **Hashtags:** 15-20 relevant tags

**Optimal Posting:** Evenings (7-9 PM ET), 3-4x per week

### TIKTOK Strategy
- **Hook:** Ultra-fast (most critical)
- **Trend:** Use trending sounds/formats
- **Text:** Large, bold, readable
- **Retention:** Keep viewer engaged every 2-3 seconds
- **CTA:** Subtle, let content speak for itself
- **Cross-Post:** Same content as Instagram Reels (9:16)

**Optimal Posting:** 6-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-10 PM ET

### YOUTUBE SHORTS Strategy
- **Hook:** First 3 seconds critical
- **Story:** Complete narrative arc
- **CTA:** Clear next step
- **Cross-Link:** Link to full YouTube videos
- **Playlist:** Group thematically

**Optimal Posting:** Friday/Monday, 6 PM ET

### YOUTUBE FULL-LENGTH Strategy
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook (0-15s): Problem statement with urgency
  2. Proof (15s-2m): Why this matters (ROI, data)
  3. Solution Overview (2-5m): What the protocol includes
  4. Deep Dive (5-10m): Detailed walkthrough
  5. Results (10-12m): Transformation stories
  6. CTA (12-15m): Product link, subscription ask
  
- **Video Elements:**
  - B-roll: Office, gym, kitchen, results
  - Graphics: Text overlays, statistics, charts
  - Voiceover: Professional, commanding tone
  - Music: Subtle, energetic background
  - Captions: Full subtitles (60% watch without sound)

**Optimal Posting:** Thursday, 6 PM ET (Evergreen)
